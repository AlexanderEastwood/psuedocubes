METHOD RELEASE · SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

CRT windows and residue bitsets for pseudocube search

Four RTX 5080s measured 0.93 trillion focused candidate positions per second, including durable coverage commits: approximately 30× the preceding pipeline. The p=619 search is ongoing; this is a method and performance release.

Canonical enumeration

Write x=uB−vA with 0≤v<B, A=701856356111039402 and B=693110504329192503. The numerical target is 1027<x≤3×1027. Coefficient-adjusted CRT wheels constrain u and v; sorted partial CRT sums enumerate bounded windows without storing the full product of residue sets. Every candidate has a unique canonical v, giving disjoint coverage partitions.

The focus split and doubly-focused enumeration build on Sorenson (2010). The initial lower boundary uses the separately attributed historical report L619,3>1027; that older computation has not been independently rerun here.

GPU/CPU fusion

Each GPU shares a prepared focus window across 64 subtiles of 100 million v. For the first four residual primes—193, 199, 211 and 223—it constructs bitsets indexed by residue phase and u position. Their intersection rejects most positions before wide-integer reconstruction. Exact bounds, carry/borrow arithmetic and remaining local filters run on surviving bits. Actual x values need about 92 bits; intermediate products can need 119 bits, so arithmetic uses two 64-bit limbs.

CPU routines independently check emitted values and verify noncube witnesses. Each GPU owns one unfinished SQLite lease; leases group 256 compute batches and retain deterministic execution-transcript hashes, all emitted values, filter populations and counts. Compressed receipts commit atomically with coverage. Recovery retains the same owner and scope and does not earn duplicate discovery credit.

Compute-only ratios and durable pipeline rates are separate measurements. A dedicated producer was slower than four self-sufficient GPU workers. Logical throughput includes positions eliminated in bulk by bitsets; it is not a measure of executed wide multiplications or occupied CUDA cores.

Qualification and limits

The deployment passed 33 CPU tests, exact comparisons of a complete 256-batch lease against the previous engine, boundary and tail controls, isolated GPU recovery before and after commit, and an isolated process exit inside a SQLite transaction. A 33-lease four-card canary audited 8,778,348,136,288 logical positions. Full small-output comparisons and known high-magnitude controls preceded the optimization.

These checks establish the stated test results and structural evidence. A valid candidate is not automatically the minimum; a matching cube count is not a proof of noncube completeness. Independent coverage replay and the historical bound remain separate questions for a final mathematical claim.
